About Us

Coco Himalaya Treks & Travels is a trusted Nepal-based trekking and adventure travel company specializing in Himalayan trekking experiences across Everest, Annapurna, Langtang, and other iconic regions of Nepal. Founded by Ukesh Bati and Diwakar Aryal, the company was established with a vision to deliver safe, authentic, and memorable journeys guided by local expertise.

With years of hands-on experience in Nepal trekking and tourism, our team offers carefully designed trekking packages, cultural tours, peak climbing, and adventure holidays that showcase the natural beauty, rich culture, and warm hospitality of Nepal. From the world-famous Everest Base Camp Trek to off-the-beaten-path Himalayan trails, we ensure every trip is well-planned, responsible, and personalized.

At Coco Himalaya Treks & Travels, we prioritize experienced local guides, safety at high altitude, and responsible tourism practices that support local communities. Our deep knowledge of the Nepal Himalayas allows us to provide reliable trekking itineraries, flexible services, and genuine cultural experiences for trekkers from around the world.

Whether you are planning your first trek in Nepal or seeking a challenging Himalayan adventure, Coco Himalaya Treks & Travels is your trusted partner for unforgettable trekking and travel experiences in Nepal.
